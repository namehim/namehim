# Removal Request Process

NameHim is a community‑driven awareness platform. All reports are user‑generated and unverified. We do not proactively investigate or endorse any claim.

If you believe your personal information has been posted on NameHim without your consent, or if you are the subject of a report and wish to request removal, please follow the process below.

## How to request removal

1. **Send an email** to namehim.app@proton.me with the subject line “Removal Request”.
2. **Include the following information**:
   - Your name.
   - The exact text or URL of the report(s) you want removed.
   - The reason for removal (e.g., you are the person named, the report is false, etc.).
   - Any supporting evidence (e.g., court records, proof of identity). This helps us make a fair decision.
3. **Review timeline** – We will acknowledge your email within 7 days. Due to limited staff (sole operator), a full review may take 14–30 days. Complex cases may take longer.

## What happens next

- After reviewing your request, we will either **remove the report** or **deny the request**.
- If we remove a report, the original poster will not be directly notified (the site is anonymous). However, they may notice the removal and resubmit their report or contact us with additional information.
- If we deny your request, you will be informed of the reason.

## If a report you submitted is removed

If you are the original poster and you believe your report was removed in error:
- You may **resubmit** the report through the normal submission form.
- You may also **email us** with additional context or evidence, and we will reconsider.

## Good‑faith moderation

This removal process is part of our good‑faith moderation. It does not mean we officially investigate every report, nor does it change the site’s disclaimer that all content is user‑generated and unverified. We simply provide a fair way for individuals to raise concerns about content on the site.

**Contact:** `moderation@namehim.app`
